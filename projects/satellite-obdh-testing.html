Satellite Communications · December 2025

Satellite OBDH System Design & Testing

Experimental testing of a satellite communication terminal, including data transfer, GNSS integration, latency, and antenna response.

Project overview

Course experimental work investigating the Loongson satellite communication terminal and its integration with the Tiantong communications system.

Methods

Functional verification of uplink and downlink messaging, BeiDou/GPS positioning uploads, latency measurements over packet sizes from 10 to 1000 bytes, and antenna pattern characterization.

Results

The report documents successful satellite-linked message exchange and GNSS data reporting, with a sample uplink confirmation time of approximately 2 seconds and downlink reception time of approximately 5 seconds.

Scope and limitations

Measurements are specific to the tested equipment and conditions, and should not be generalized to all satellite OBDH implementations.
